#### [00_prototype_alpha](./00_prototype_alpha/readme.md) — Ironless Planar Linear Motor

<div align="center">
    <img src="../05_media/02_prototype_alpha/02_experimental/side_view_on_test_stand.jpg" alt="Side view on stand" style="max-width: 600px">
    <br>
    <em>Prototype Alpha. Side view on test stand without encoder wiring.</em>
</div>

<br>

Prototype Alpha is an `ironless planar linear motor` with a polylactic acid (PLA) armature featuring `6` slots, hand wound using `0.2 mm` diameter enameled copper wire and `5 mm` wide Kapton tape, with `2` slots in-series per phase (WYE). The stator, similar to the armature, was printed in PLA and had `4` pole pairs per armature length and `10` pole pairs total.

### [01_prototype_beta](./01_prototype_beta/readme.md) — Ironless Tubular Linear Motor (WIP)

<div align="center">
<img src="../05_media/03_prototype_beta/rev_2/side_profile_cad.png" alt="Side profile" style="max-width: 600px">
<br>
<em>Prototype Beta revision 2: Radial heat-sink design study (aluminum 6061, eddy current analysis).</em>
</div>

<br>

Prototype Beta is a series of thermal management studies for an `ironless tubular linear motor`, with a final implementation and validation of the thermal management strategy developed through the `rev 1` and `rev 2` studies.

`Rev 3` will most likely use an outer radial heat sink made of `aluminum`, with coils potted into that heat sink and the final inner bore hole being machined out.

Reference: [Defining constraints and success metrics for prototype beta rev 3](https://github.com/wgbowley/OpenLSM/issues/5)
